## 🔹 Role Comparison: DE vs DA vs DS

| Role | Focus | Key Skills |
|---|---|---|
| **Data Engineer** | Collect & store data | Strong programming, DB knowledge, Big Data (Hadoop), ETL tools |
| **Data Analyst** | Clean & make useful insights | Excel, CSV, Power BI, Tableau, basic Python & maths |
| **Data Scientist** | Prediction & modeling | Stats, ML, Python, model building |

**Earning potential (general trend):** `DS > DE > DA`
